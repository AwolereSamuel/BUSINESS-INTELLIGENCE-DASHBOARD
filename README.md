# BUSINESS-INTELLIGENCE-DASHBOARD
PROJECT DESCRIPTION: 

An interactive Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard developed to analyse business performance, sales trends, profitability, product performance, customer segments, regional performance, and employee sales contributions for a retail organisation.
The dashboard transforms business data into actionable insights that can support management decision-making, performance monitoring, and strategic planning.

# PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project focuses on developing a Business Performance Intelligence Dashboard that enables management to understand:

- Overall sales and revenue performance.

- Profitability and profit margins.

- Product and category performance.

- Customer and regional contributions.

- Salesperson performance.

- Business opportunities and underperforming areas.

The dashboard uses Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to prepare, analyse, and visualise business data through interactive report pages.

# OBJECTIVES
1 Analyze total sales, quantity, costs, and profit.

2 Monitor profit margins and sales growth.

3 Identify high-performing products and categories.

4 Analyze regional and customer performance.

5 Evaluate salesperson contributions.

6 Identify products or segments requiring management attention.

7 Present business insights through interactive Power BI reports.

8 Support data-driven business decision-making.

# TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES
- Microsoft Power BI: Dashboard development and data visualisation.

- Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation.

- DAX: Measures and business calculations.

- Data Modelling: Relationships between fact and dimension tables.

- Microsoft Excel: Data handling and preparation.

# KEY FEATURES 
1. Data Preparation

- Reviewed and prepared business data for analysis.

- Organised data into fact and dimension tables.

- Worked with sales, customer, product, employee, inventory, expense, and target data.

- Prepared the data model for reporting and analysis.


2. KPI Analysis

The dashboard includes performance indicators such as:

- Total Sales

- Total Quantity

- Total Cost

- Total Profit

- Profit Margin

- Total Orders

- Average Order Value

- Previous Month Sales

- Sales Growth

- Previous Year Sales


3. Dashboard Pages

- Executive Overview: Provides a summary of the organization's key performance indicators and overall business performance.

- Sales & Profit Analysis: Examines sales and profitability across products, categories, regions, and other business dimensions.

- Management Analysis: Supports the identification of business opportunities, underperforming areas, products requiring attention, and potential management actions.

- Product Tooltip: Provides additional product-level information, including sales, profit, profit margin, quantity, and order-related metrics when users interact with dashboard visuals.


4. Interactivity

- Report-page navigation

- Interactive visual filtering

- Page navigation buttons

- Product-level report tooltips

- Comparative business analysis

# RESULTS

The dashboard provides an interactive reporting environment for evaluating business performance across multiple dimensions. The analysis is designed to help management:

- Monitor sales and profitability.

- Compare product and category performance.

- Identify strong and weak business segments.

- Evaluate sales performance across regions and employees.

- Investigate products with high sales but comparatively weak profitability.

- Support evidence-based business recommendations.
